About & Controls

An endless skyline of neo-gothic towers and cathedral spires, half-swallowed by a stormy ocean. Everything is drawn in real time with a single raymarched fragment shader: analytic Gerstner-style waves, physically-based Fresnel water, soft shadows, ambient occlusion, atmospheric haze, an ACES-tonemapped sky, lightning and rain.

Mouse & keyboard

  • Drag the canvas — look around (orbit the camera).
  • Scroll / pinch on canvas — raise or lower the camera.
  • Tab — move between every control. Space/Enter — activate buttons.
  • ← → on a focused slider — fine adjust; Home/End jump to ends.
  • P pause/play · F fullscreen · H this help.

Accessibility

  • Every slider and toggle is keyboard-operable with a visible focus ring and a screen-reader label.
  • Reduce motion freezes the camera fly-through and calms the waves.
  • Reduce transparency turns the frosted-glass panels into solid high-contrast surfaces (auto-enabled if your OS requests it).
  • The canvas exposes a descriptive text alternative for screen readers.

Run it in twigl.app

  • Press Copy twigl shader. Open twigl.app, choose the classic mode, paste, and run. The exported code bakes in your current sliders as constants and uses twigl's built-in resolution, mouse and time uniforms.
