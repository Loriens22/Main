？ How it works

The building. A modern 4-storey co-op built to real residential proportions (2.8 m ceilings, 0.2 m slabs, code-sized rooms). Floors 1–3 hold three flats each; the top floor holds two large maisonettes (double-height duplexes with internal stairs). Every flat has at least a bedroom, a bathroom with WC, a kitchen and a living hall — bigger flats get extra rooms.

Neighbors. Tap ＋ New neighbor to sculpt body shape, clothes & name, write a personality, and optionally wire up the NVIDIA API with custom instructions. Confirm, and they move into the flat you chose.

Encounters. When two neighbors meet, a 💬 cloud pops above them with model-generated talk. Tap the cloud to read the whole conversation. Clashing personalities cause scandals and even fights.

Camera. Drag to orbit, pinch/scroll to zoom, two fingers to pan. Use View to hide the roof, cut walls down, explode the floors, or isolate one level.
