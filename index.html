The building. A modern 4-storey co-op at real residential proportions (2.8 m ceilings, 0.2 m slabs, code-sized rooms) with a detailed clad facade, pilasters, string courses, sills, balconies, a glazed lobby, landscaped grounds and a rooftop plant. Floors 1–3 hold three flats each; the top floor holds two large maisonettes (double-height duplexes with internal stairs). Every flat has at least a bedroom, a bathroom with WC, a kitchen and a living hall — bigger flats get extra rooms — all furnished.

See inside. Use Peek inside (or the View panel) to drop the roof & ceilings, slide a section cut down through the building, fade the walls see-through, explode the floors apart, or isolate one level.

Neighbors. Tap ＋ New neighbor to sculpt body shape, clothes & name, write a personality, and optionally wire up the NVIDIA API (with a CORS proxy + custom instructions) for true model-reasoned dialogue. Use Test connection first.

Encounters. When two neighbors meet, a 💬 cloud appears. Tap it to read the whole conversation — including which engine generated it. Neighbors remember past run-ins, so feuds escalate into scandals and fights.
