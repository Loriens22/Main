DRIVER'S NOTES
How to drive sweetly
● Ease the power on. Slamming W or S rattles the cabin and drains leg comfort.
● Slow into the elevated curves. Above ~55 km/h the bend starts throwing people about.
● Crosswind on the bridges. When the pill on the right turns gold, hold A or D against the gust — line the white trim pip up with the gold wind pip.
● Stop on the mark. Coast to a halt inside the lantern-lit stopping zone for the arrival bonus.
● Keep the streak. Every smooth stretch adds to it; a rough one breaks it.
