DRIVER'S NOTES
How to drive sweetly
● Ease the power on. Slamming W or S rattles the cabin and drains leg comfort.
● Slow into the elevated curves. Push a tight bend and someone loses their footing — every accident costs comfort and tips.
● Crosswind on the bridges. When the pill on the right turns gold, hold A or D against the gust — line the white trim pip up with the gold wind pip.
● Mind the banner. The line hands you a job: spore drift at Mycelia, a lantern on the rails at the Hollow, a squall, a passenger asleep. Hold it for the few seconds it asks and the cabin pays you for it.
● Ring the bell (B) once Oliver has fitted one. It settles everybody — and shifts whatever has climbed onto the roof.
● Stop on the mark, and don't shut the doors on anyone still boarding.
● Keep the streak. Every smooth stretch adds to it; a rough one breaks it.
