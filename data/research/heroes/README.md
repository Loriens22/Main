# Hero buildings of line 7 and the city extras — sources and estimates

The NDK, the Millennium Center, the NDK administrative tower (bTV Media Group), Bulgaria Mall and the BILLA
store at бул. „България“ 55 are modelled in Blender (`tools/blender/hero/*.py`) on their OpenStreetMap
footprints. Their appearance follows **photographs supplied by the user** (street-level captures of August
2024 and June 2022, aerial and night views of the NDK and a rendering of its renovation). The photographs are
used only as visual reference. No photograph or other imagery is used as a texture, and every texture
is drawn procedurally. The usual photo hosts (Wikimedia, Flickr, the owners' sites) are blocked from the build
environment, so the written facts come from search summaries of the pages listed below.

## NDK — Национален дворец на културата

- Opened 31 March 1981 for the 1300th anniversary of Bulgaria; design team led by Alexander Barov, structure
  by Bogdan Atanasov, surroundings by Atanas Agura; 8 floors above ground and 3 below, about 123 000 m²; its
  height is given as 51 m ([bg.wikipedia](https://bg.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9D%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BD_%D0%B4%D0%B2%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%86_%D0%BD%D0%B0_%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BB%D1%82%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B0),
  [noblehouse.bg](https://noblehouse.bg/blog/nacionalen-dvorec-na-kulturata),
  [Столица.bg](https://stolica.bg/sofia-tur/arhitekturni-pametnitsi/sgradata-na-ndk)).
- "A mass of concrete and glass"; the stylized sun of Georgi Chapkanov high on the facade; a park with
  fountains in front ([visitsofia.bg](https://www.visitsofia.bg/en/cityinfrastructure/what-to-see/architectural-monuments/the-building-of-the-national-palace-of-culture),
  [tourism.government.bg](https://www.tourism.government.bg/en/tourist-destinations/2799/5785),
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Palace_of_Culture)).
- The park and its fountains were renovated in 2014–2015 for about 11.5 million leva; the square in front,
  пл. „България“, has 12 500 m² ([Capital 2013](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2013/11/17/2184361_parkut_okolo_ndk_shte_se_preobraziava/),
  [Capital 2016](https://www.capital.bg/politika_i_ikonomika/bulgaria/2016/10/27/2852116_dji_pi_grup_nai-veroiatno_shte_remontira/)).
- **From the photographs:** the following are modelled from the photographs.
  - The stone corner towers and the bronze glass behind white fins with stepped lower ends.
  - The ledge band, the white sun panel and the recessed upper storey under a dark cornice.
  - The vaulted skylights, the copper roofs, the stepped terraces with planting and the octagonal fountain
    court with the sphere.
  - The flags, and the cascade with paired lit jets, herringbone edges, planting beds and globe lamps.
- **Estimated:** storey and band heights (ledge 7.0–8.6 m, top band 31.5–33.5 m, cornice 38.9 m), the fin
  rhythm (about 3 m), the roof shapes and the stage towers' heights (44.5 and 46 m), the tiers of the fountain
  court. The OSM main body is 40 m.

## Millennium Center (бул. „Витоша“ 89Б)

- Three towers on a five-storey base: the 32-storey Grand Hotel Millennium Sofia, a residential and an office
  tower; 40 000 m² of glass "made up of geometric forms", grey and white elements; architect Amfion
  ([Reynaers](https://www.reynaers.com/en/get-inspired/aluminium-project-references/millennium-centre-towers),
  [Building of the Year](https://www.buildingoftheyear.bg/en/buildings/view/763/Millennium-Centre.html),
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Center_(Sofia))). The hotel is given as 112 m; sources
  differ on the floors of the other two towers (18 and 24).
- **From the photographs:** the hotel's white zig-zag frame rising above the roof, the staggered white
  ledges, the space-frame lattice with ball nodes over faceted glass, the white fins, the canopy on V-columns
  with MILLENNIUM SOFIA, the flags and the shop signs (OZONE, GALLERY, VIENNA, SPECTRUM, the casino); the
  office tower's white floor bands and crown with the ДЗИ / ОББ letters; the residential tower's balconies.
- **Estimated:** base 21 m, hotel storeys 3.37 m to 112 m, office tower 78 m (OSM 77.8 m), residential
  tower 75 m (OSM 74.6 m); the lattice geometry and the rooftop helipad's layout (a rooftop helipad is
  mentioned in listings).

## NDK administrative tower — bTV Media Group

- OSM: „Административна сграда на НДК“, 54.6 m; the place data puts bTV at the NDK address.
- **From the photographs:**
  - A precast concrete office tower: deep parapet bands over ribbon windows, with air-conditioning units.
  - Storeys stepping out and in, so the corners read serrated.
  - On the roof, an open frame of beams carrying the two blue "b" squares and "Media Group".
- **Estimated:** ground floor 4.2 m, 14 storeys of 3.3 m, the band profile.

## Bulgaria Mall (бул. „България“ 69)

- Opened 1 December 2012; MYS Architects with Studio 17.5; developer MRPI
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgaria_Mall), [Salamanca Group](https://salamanca-group.com/?p=2461),
  [Capital](https://www.capital.bg/1953734/)). Descriptions found earlier mention a double-skin glazed
  entrance with LED lighting, stainless "Bulgaria Mall" letters along бул. „Тодор Каблешков“ and a 36 m
  glazed atrium.
- **From the photographs:**
  - The blue glass towers and slab, with stacks of terraces.
  - The pixel-clad podium, its white colonnade top floor and fascia with TECHNOPOLIS and BILLA, and the
    shop signs (RESERVED, terranova, LC WAIKIKI, GUESS).
  - The white-framed glass entrance block with the BULGARIA MALL canopy, the café on its ground floor and
    the freestanding letters along Каблешков.
  - The digital billboard.
- **Estimated:** podium 17 m and office storeys 3.6 m from the photographs and the storey counts (slab 56.6 m,
  North Tower 63.8 m, South Tower 85.4 m; OSM gives 45.8, 56 and 85 m); entrance block 29 m.
- The entrance block was not mapped as a building part; it is rebuilt from the rest of the building outline.

## BILLA (бул. „България“ 55)

- OSM way 203333240 (via Overture): a two-storey retail building of about 4 700 m² with its pylon (8 m) and
  two raised canopies mapped as parts; the place data puts BILLA, a pharmacy and an insurance broker at
  бул. „България“ 55.
- It was missing from the game: the world builder dropped any building that had a ground-level part, and here
  the only one was the pylon. `tools/build_world.py` now keeps the rest of the outline when the parts leave
  most of it uncovered (40 buildings on line 7 regained their missing part).
- **From the photographs:**
  - Silver-grey cladding and the BILLA НА БЪЛГАРИЯ lettering on the corner.
  - A poster and the red "Спестявайте с BILLA Card" banner, white umbrellas by the doors.
  - The grey cylinder pylon with BILLA letters and its tenants' signs (ОПТИКА, SOpharmacy, speedy).
  - The yellow sculpture on the lamp post.
- **Estimated:**
  - Store height 8.6 m and pylon height 11 m.
  - The entrance position.
  - The poster artwork, which is generic.

## City extras (`src/world/CityExtras.js`, `tools/blender/kit_street.py`)

- Playground equipment in the OSM playground areas and park furniture in the parks and gardens near the
  route are generic pieces typical of Sofia's housing estates and parks (the wooden gazebo — беседка —,
  the drinking fountain — чешма —, concrete table-tennis tables, outdoor fitness bars). Their number scales
  with the area; their positions are chosen clear of paths, trees and buildings and are not surveyed.
- Shop props follow each real shop's kind as read from its name (cafés get terraces, groceries fruit stands,
  pharmacies the green cross, banks an ATM); they are illustrative, not surveyed.
- Murals on blank gable walls of tall slab blocks are **original procedural artwork**, not reproductions of
  existing murals: a folk-embroidery pattern, Vitosha at sunrise, colour blocks, roses, a tram at night, birds.
