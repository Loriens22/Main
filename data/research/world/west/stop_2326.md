## What a person at the pole sees

- **Across the road, 50-100 m left-ahead: the former Hotel Pliska.** A 58.6 m slab tower (OSM 18 levels; sources 14-16 floors) standing on a two-storey zig-zag podium, with its long side (36.5 m) running at right angles to the boulevard and its narrow 18.7 m end towards the road. Opened 1969 by Balkantourist (hotel for Balkan Air passengers), closed October 2012, bought by CCB in 2013, **reopened in April 2015 as CCB's headquarters** with a new hung aluminium-glass curtain-wall facade. The red neon "ПЛИСКА" roof sign of the hotel era cannot be assumed to exist now.
- **Behind the pole:** the paved plaza, trees, shop pavilions (casino SESAME Pliska, pharmacy, Yettel shop, grill at No. 14 are on the SW side) and the Izток block bl. 10 (7 storeys, 76 m long, panel). The underpass stairs are covered with long flat canopies.
- **Behind (NW):** Billa supermarket (2 storeys, 50 x 28 m, 90 m back), dm, Pepco and Fredi pavilions along the service lane, the Radoy Ralin monument (mapped at (493.7,-740.9)).
- **Ahead:** an unbroken 6-8 storey wall of 1970s blocks on both sides, the boulevard continues with 3 + 3 lanes to the Izток stop 800 m later.

## Pliska junction

OSM shows no signalised crossing at the pole itself: the Pliska underpass is the pedestrian crossing, signals/crossings appear 14 m ahead (crossing a=-4 at +13.4). The service lanes meet the main carriageways through gaps in the guard fence.
