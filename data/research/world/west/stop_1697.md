## How the B bus arrives (the last 640 m of direction B)

From the Eagles' Bridge the bus drives NW along бул. Цар Освободител on **yellow paving** (OSM yellow_brick, 15 m, two-way; a 5 m one-way yellow slip road runs 21 m to the right), with a big park (OSM park + plaza, 9-350 m deep: Doctors' Garden / Borisova gradina side) on the left, a line of 7-storey 1960s blocks (w2509/2511/2513, 21.6 m) and then the south wing of Sofia University (OSM parts 1895 and 1894, 18.6 m, 26 m from the centreline) on the right. At about (-1450,-2682) it turns right (north) onto **бул. Васил Левски** (15 m, ordinary asphalt, NOT yellow). For the next 140 m the university frontage is on the right at 11-16 m from the centreline (3-8 m behind the footway): OSM parts 1901 (central/Rectorate block, 6 levels, 20.2 m, west face at x = -1424), 1902 (17 m), then the north wing 1898 (18.6 m) and 1899. On the left a front garden ("Градинката пред СУ": lawn, flower beds, garden polygons at -17 .. -50 m), the plaza with the monument of St Kliment Ohridski at (-1469,-2840) and the paved plaza (-1479,-2750). At (-1419,-2830) the bus turns right (east-south-east) onto Шипка and stops at the pole 14 m later (terminalLinks BtoA end (-1335.6,-2808.4)).

Rectorate facade (what the B bus passes): eclectic with predominantly Baroque forms (Milanov's revision, 1924-34); a "triumphal arch" parade entrance flanked by two lions with statues of Socrates and Plato between them (one source - tentative); on both sides of the parade staircase two seated bronze figures of the donors Evlogi and Hristo Georgiev (sculptor Kiril Shivarov); sculptural decoration by Mihail Parashchuk, Lyuben Dimitrov and Lyubomir Dalchev; floor area 18,624 m2, 324 rooms. Confidence on exact portal form: low. The previous game model (six-column portico with pediment) is NOT supported by any source.

## What a person standing at the pole sees

Facing along the bus heading (east-south-east, down Шипка):

- **Behind the pole (south, right of the road): the north wing of Sofia University.** The OSM building part (18.6 m high) is a strip about 100 m long and 26 m deep whose north face is the line (-1388,-2812) -> (-1325,-2795): that line is only 2.5-5 m behind the pole, i.e. the wall rises from the back of the pavement. The OSM "fence" line along the same line is the building frontage / low railing. This wing was finished in 1985 (built 1972-85) and "re-faced the central Rectorate" (bg.wikipedia search snippet). Treat as a long modernist, stone-coloured slab, 5 floors, flat roof, regular window grid, a few entrances.
- **The Rectorate itself** (6-level central block, OSM part 20.2 m) is 65-90 m to the south-west behind the wing. It faces Цар Освободител / Васил Левски, not Шипка, so from the pole it is hidden. Its four-column portico, Georgiev brothers statues and monument of St Kliment (at about (-1469,-2840), 137 m WSW) are on the other side.
- **Left of the road (north):** a footway/steps zone (OSM steps at (-1352,-2836)->(-1344,-2835), footways to (-1286,-2822)) then the National Library (НБКМ, 4 levels, 1953, Vasilyov & Tsolov) 30-70 m north; its tall mass is further NNW. A park patch with trees (OSM park at (-1277,-2870)) lies between.
- **Ahead on the right (60 m):** the corner building Шипка 6 (5 levels, 46 x 31 m at (-1278,-2763)), then the 5-storey apartment blocks Шипка 12 / Кракра 12-14 with ground-floor shops (PopShop tobacco, Foto Svyat, 100 Grams of Sweets, florist "Изненада", optician Onyx), then the hotel Crystal Palace (Шипка 14, 6-7 floors) at x about -1196.
- **At the junction 45 m ahead** the small residential street ул. Св. Климент Охридски (5 m, one-way) comes in from the south at (-1291,-2797); it separates the university from Шипка 6.
- **Cafe "При Музея"** is mapped 16 m from the pole at (-1322,-2810) (a kiosk/pavilion type café on the pavement).
