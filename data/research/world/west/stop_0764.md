## The name

The stop is called "НСБАЛ по онкология" but the hospital (National Specialised Hospital for Active Treatment of Oncology, ul. Plovdivsko pole 6, in zh.k. Musagenitsa; central entrance from бул. Климент Охридски; neighbour of the haematology hospital SBALHZ) lies 130-400 m further along the route (see landmarks.json "oncology_campus"), not at the pole. The pole is at the metro station and the nearest buildings are the metro pavilions, a petrol station and offices.

## What a person at the pole sees

- **Behind the pole:** the low glazed surface pavilion of metro station G. M. Dimitrov (opened 8 May 2009, line M1; M4 later; architect Kr. Andreev; inside brown/beige granite panels and etalbond ceilings) with its steps, bike racks and vending kiosks. A second pavilion at (404.7,616.7).
- **Left (east, across the junction):** the road junction with signals, then the Vitosha Park hotel / Studentski grad frontage (mapped 48 m away at (478.7,658.7) - a hostel/educational block), the UNWE department sign, the tower cluster further north-west.
- **Ahead:** the boulevard bends south-south-east; the oncology hospital campus starts on the LEFT about 130 m ahead (OSM hospital polygon east of the road), after the OMV petrol station and the scrub strip.
- **Towers:** three towers are mapped by OSM 230-290 m north-west of the route at (425,372) 71.4 m / 22 levels, (374,375) 61.8 m / 19 levels and (309,371) 49 m / 15 levels - the "twin skyscrapers" of the 2018 plan (office tower 106 m "NV Tower" by Minstroy, 22-storey 75 m residential block by Artex) may be part of this cluster; construction status unconfirmed.
