Naming note: the stop is named after the Georgi Rakovski Military Academy, but the Academy's main building (OSM 4692 m2 "university" polygon, centroid (-369,-2476)) and its walled park are 300 m to the north-east. From the pole only the Academy park trees and wall are seen (OSM wall (-655,-2540)->(-580,-2602), 60-90 m north-east of the pole, beyond the junction). Main building: 1888-1894, Czech architect Antonin Kolar, "fortress-like, arched windows, military ornaments on the roof like battle towers, clock on the east facade" (explorerbulgaria.com via search snippet); further 1938-42 building by architect Alexander Dubovnik; park attributed to Friedrich Grunanger (single source, low confidence). Academy address: бул. Евлоги и Христо Георгиеви 82.

## What a person at the pole sees

- **Directly behind (south): a row of 3-5 storey pre-war town buildings with ground-floor shops at Shipka 52 (bakery "Пекарна Симид" 9 m from the pole, boutique Pentagram), a 3-storey house, and behind them villas.** This is the Oborishte quarter: 1930s-built brick/plaster apartment buildings and older houses (bazar.bg listings for the quarter state brick construction, year 1930).
- **Across Shipka (north):** the 5-storey blocks at Shipka 35 / 41 (shops: butcher "Нашата Месарница", hardware REA 4, a resin-furniture studio, a maths-school office) and, behind them, Prof. Asen Zlatarov restaurants (Troika, La Pastaria).
- **Ahead (east, 17 m):** the road ends in the junction. Shipka continues east as a 9.5 m two-way street (OSM) towards the Academy park; Prof. Asen Zlatarov goes north (the bus turns left onto it) and south; Evlogi Georgiev passes in a loop (secondary, one-way, 7.5 m, (-649,-2610) -> (-678,-2590)). Traffic signals mapped at (-653,-2623) and (-631,-2617); four mapped crossings.
- **Small plaza on the right-hand corner: Площад "Вацлав Хавел"** ((-664,-2613), about 340 m2). Named 2020; the sculpture "Сърцето пътува" (The Heart Travels) by Kurt Gebauer was unveiled there (BTA: 31 October 2024). It is a copy of the Prague heart (large grey stone-looking heart); size/material of the Sofia copy not confirmed. Three benches (-665.8,-2613.6), (-661.4,-2616.8), (-660.9,-2611.7) and an OSM artwork at (-662.1,-2615.6) 30 m from the pole; litter container at (-664,-2642) and recycling at (-664,-2636.6).
- Behind the plaza, on the other side of Evlogi Georgiev, the Military Academy wall and tall plane/lime trees of the park.

## Landmarks passed between the terminal and this stop (Shipka, s = 0..671)

- Shipka is a 6 m one-way street. On the left (north) in the first 150 m: a park strip with trees and the NBKM/Oborishte buildings; from x = -1170 the street is lined on both sides with 4-6 storey plastered apartment blocks and hotels (Crystal Palace, Шипка 14).
- **Шипка 34 - Russian Cultural-Informational Centre** (former Soviet House of Science and Culture, 1971-75, architects Georgi Berberov and Ivo Tsolov, per sofiaplan.bg doc via search) - a large 5-level public building ((-979,-2669), 2846 m2 footprint, 15.6 m) on the right, set back about 40 m from the road behind a garden. Modernist; facade not verified.
- Detached villas / embassy houses (South African embassy house at (-981,-2730), "Къщата на архитекта Георги Фингов" at (-887,-2669)) on the left between x = -1000 and -850: 2-3 storey 1920s-30s villas with tile roofs and gardens.
- Slaveykov square junction at about x = -860 (Площад Петко Р. Славейков 7 is a gallery address).
- The 38th school (Vasil Aprilov) with two pitches on the right-hand side at x = -800.
