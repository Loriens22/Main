# Stop 1258 - Площад на авиацията (direction B only)

Direction B, s = 6216 m. Direction A does not stop here: route A leaves Tsarigradsko shosse at (1067,-200), 300 m before the square, onto the connector to Dr G. M. Dimitrov.

## What the square is

"Площад на авиацията" (also "Площад Авиационен", former "Карл Винаров", the "4-ти километър" junction) is a **grade-separated interchange**, not a classical roundabout: the Tsarigradsko shosse carriageways (OSM trunk, 80 km/h, flags oneway) pass diagonally through it ((1143,-162) -> (1251,-60) -> (1372,57) -> (1442,127) and back), and the route-B loop road (OSM primary 15 m two-way) curls around its western side. Named after the Bulgarian Air Force buildings nearby. B path: Dr G. M. Dimitrov east to (1250,89) -> (1296,90) -> up (1340,85), (1385,45), (1393,8), (1390,-18), (1368,-55) -> stop -> WNW along (1322,-79), (1281,-86), (1226,-108) -> connector to Tsarigradsko shosse at (1104,-214).

## What a person at the pole sees

- **The Aviation Monument ("Паметник на авиацията и военновъздушните сили"), 75-110 m to the north-north-east**, between the arena and the military dormitories. Opened 16 October 2012 for the 100th anniversary of the first Bulgarian combat flight (Lt Radul Milkov and Prodan Tarakchiev). Sculptor Dimitar Chonov, architect Irina Krushkova. Three parts (sources: RIM Sofia, bg.wikipedia, opoznai.bg):
  - central tapering pillar of rectangular section, max. height **10.60 m**, clad in **grey granite**;
  - on top a **brass eagle** (~300 kg, 1.50 m high, 1.10 m wide, **wingspan 2.50 m**), symbol of the Air Force;
  - two side bodies shaped like wings, hyperbolic form, **4.60 m and 3.00 m high**. OSM probably carries them as two unnamed footprints (5.3 x 3.5 m at (1395.6,-141.9) and 6.8 x 4.5 m at (1393.1,-180.7)); the pillar then stands between them at about (1394,-161).
  - There is NO confirmed aircraft on a pylon (one source claims a propeller aircraft on a pylon right of the monument, a second search found none: treat as unconfirmed, do not model).
- **Behind the monument (north-north-east, 180 m): Arena 8888 Sofia** (ex Arena Armeec), 137 x 113 m, opened 2011.
- **Ahead-left across the loop road:** the open grass, the OMV petrol station and "The Mall" shopping centre (Tsarigradsko 115B) mapped at (1312,6)/(1276,-17), 90-100 m to the south-west, and the Q Center office block (9 storeys, glass) at (1205,22).
- **Right/north:** military compound (unit 54800 / National Guard, behind a fence and guard house on bul. Asen Yordanov) and tall tree belts.
- Pavement of the loop is plain asphalt; a pedestrian crossing and give-way marks are mapped at (1339.9,-86.8) / (1343.2,-88.7) (the exit onto Asen Yordanov).
