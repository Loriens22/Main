# ISLAND PROTOCOL: PRESIDENTIAL EXTRACTION
## Story Bible — Agent A (Narrative)

**Logline:** The rescue was the easy part. One operator, one twenty-one-year-old
asset, eleven kilometres of hostile island, and a helicopter that will not wait.

**Tagline (title card):** *You already found her. Now get her off the rock.*

---

## 1. TONE AND RULES OF THE WORLD

Terse. Wet. Under-lit. The game is a corridor of small emergencies. Nobody
monologues. The plot arrives in three ways only:

1. **Radio chatter** — ANVIL and CARRION-6 in the ear, clipped, procedural,
   occasionally human when the channel is quiet.
2. **Found documents** — memos, logs, evidence tags, payroll disputes. Artifacts,
   not lore dumps. They have reference numbers and bad handwriting.
3. **Combat barks and one-to-three-line exchanges** — Elena and Callen say things
   under load. Never a speech. Never during a fight unless it is under six words.

Hard writing rules:
- No character explains the parasite. The reader assembles it.
- Callen never says anything that could not be said with a rifle in his hands.
- Elena's speech register is a **health bar for her mind**. Panic is clipped,
  breathy, repetitive. Calm is dry and dark. She gets funnier as she gets braver.
- No profanity escalation as a substitute for stakes.
- The word "asset" is a weapon the script uses against the player. It stops being
  used, on purpose, at a specific moment, and the player should feel it.

---

## 2. PREMISE

**Isla Verdadera** sits 340 km east-southeast of the Carolina shelf, inside a
permanent low-pressure scar the charts call the Cortadura. It has been a fishing
village, a quarantine, a drowned village, a monastery ossuary, and finally a
biological research station operated by ARGOS BIOSCIENCE under a black-budget
DoD contract.

Under the station is **VERDIGRIS**: an organism dredged out of the Cortadura
Trench in 1961 and, in every meaningful sense, still down there. It colonises a
host through an ovule implanted at the brainstem. It does not kill. It *edits*.
Hosts keep their language, their loyalties, their muscle memory, and lose the
part that says no.

**LOS SEMBRADORES** — the Sowers — are what remains of the 1974 village. They
believe Verdigris is a covenant, that the world is a fallow field, and that a
planting requires a vessel the world cannot ignore.

Eleven days ago they took **ELENA VOSS**, twenty-one, marine biology graduate
student, off the research vessel *Corrigan* during a benthic survey. She is the
daughter of the President of the United States. Codename **PHOENIX**.

The United States sent one man, because one man fits inside a submersible
delivery vehicle and a diplomatic denial.

**The game begins ninety seconds after Callen Reyes cuts the lock on her cell.**

## 3. CAST

### CALLEN REYES — "WARDEN"
Thirty-eight. USSTRATCOM Special Activities, detached. Ten years of work that
does not appear in any citation. Speaks in imperative sentences. Not cold —
*narrowed*. He is very good at exactly one thing and has organised a life around
being needed for it.

He begins the mission treating Elena as freight: *the asset, the package, the
cargo*. This is not cruelty, it is a load-bearing wall. If she is a person, he
has to survive the arithmetic of failing her. He has failed a package before.
He does not talk about it and the script never explains it — one document and
one line of Kowalski's do the work.

**Arc:** asset -> she -> Elena. The turn happens in the catacombs, out loud.

### ELENA VOSS — "PHOENIX"
Twenty-one. Grad student, benthic ecology. Grew up with agents in the driveway
and learned early how to be small and agreeable to keep a room calm. That skill
is why she survived eleven days in a cell, and it is the exact skill she has to
break to survive tonight.

She has a four-centimetre sutured incision at the base of her skull.

**Arc, four legible tiers:**
1. **PANIC** (Prologue–early Act 1) — fragments, repetition, apologies. "Okay.
   Okay. I'm okay. I'm sorry. Okay."
2. **COMPLIANCE** (Act 1) — she does exactly what she is told, immediately,
   with nothing added. Her lines shorten to acknowledgements. This is not
   recovery, it is shock wearing a uniform. "Behind you. Behind you."
3. **COMPETENCE** (Act 2) — she starts contributing information. Counts rounds.
   Reads the tide. Uses her actual education. Makes her first joke, badly.
4. **AGENCY** (Act 3) — she gives orders, refuses one of his, and chooses to
   stay for someone else. She hands him magazines before he asks.

### MAJ. IRENE KOWALSKI — "ANVIL"
Handler, aboard the *Fitzgerald* 90 km out. Runs the clock and the lie. She is
the only person who talks to Callen like he is a colleague and not a tool, and
she is also the person who will order him to leave Elena if the ovule matures.
Both of those are sincere.

### WO1 MARC DUBOIS — "CARRION-6"
Pilot. Nightstalker airframe, no callsign vanity, terrible jokes, immaculate
airmanship. Represents *outside* — every transmission from him is oxygen. He
will be flying into a storm the Air Force has red-lined.

### FATHER AMADOR SERRANO
Seventy-one. Was the village schoolteacher in 1974. Host to a mature strain —
the oldest continuous colonisation on record. Speaks with enormous patience and
zero doubt. He does not hate Callen; he thinks Callen is weather. He is
genuinely gentle with Elena, which is worse.

### COL. VIKTOR MERSE
Contracted security, six years on the ARGOS gate. Sold the island's access
architecture to Serrano for a lump sum, was paid forty percent, and has spent
three months trying to invoice a cult. He is not a believer. He is worse: an
administrator with a rifle. His men are unpaid, cold, and increasingly the
first course.

### DR. MIREN HALDANE
Chief researcher, Station Verdadera. She engineered strain V-09 for vertical
stability — a parasite that breeds true. She did it because uncontrolled
Verdigris burns through a host in nine days and takes a coastline with it, and a
*stable* strain can be studied, bounded, killed. She is telling the truth. She is
also the reason Elena has an incision.

She has an inhibitor. She has two doses. She will not hand them over for free.

**Haldane is the only genuine gray:** every terrible thing she did has a defensible
paper trail, and the paper trail is the horror.

## 4. THE ORGANISM (writers' reference — never stated aloud)

- **Vector:** an ovule, roughly the size of an almond, seated against the
  brainstem. Surgical or oral-regurgitant delivery.
- **Latency:** dormant until a *sowing* — a specific low-frequency vocalisation.
  Serrano is a living tuning fork. This is why killing Serrano matters more than
  killing the strain.
- **Colonisation:** 6–40 hours. Host retains speech and skills. Pain response
  drops. Group cohesion rises. Late-stage hosts share a ranging sense.
- **Plaga expression:** under lethal cranial trauma, the organism emerges as a
  secondary head or limb structure to protect its investment. This is the
  "second growth" the mercenaries have a word for.
- **Weakness:** ultraviolet at 265 nm, sustained; and the loss of the mature
  broadcaster.
- **Colour:** everything it touches goes the green-black of oxidised copper.
  Hence the name on the containment placards — VERDIGRIS.

## 5. STRUCTURE

Total runtime target: 4.5–6 hours. Extraction clock starts at Act 3 open.

### PROLOGUE — "GROUND TRUTH"
**Sections:** `cells`, `sublevel`, `lift_shaft`
**Objective chain:** reach the service lift -> restore lift power -> hold the
safe room -> ride to the surface levels.

Pistol only, eleven rounds, one flashlight with a bad contact. Teaches: move,
aim, the difference between a shot and a hit, the Elena command wheel
(FOLLOW / STAY / HIDE / COME / INTERACT), and grabs.

Story beats: the cell block ledger with Elena's designation on it; the first
thing that gets up after being shot; Kowalski's first check-in confirming the
window is not what they briefed.

**Closing beat:** Callen reports the asset is mobile. Elena, behind him, says
her own name out loud to a stranger's radio, like she is checking it still works.

### ACT I — "FACILITY BREACH"
**Sections:** `west_labs`, `containment_b`, `vent_warrens`, `power_station`,
`loading_yard`
**Objective chain:** cross west research -> seal the ruptured cryo line ->
cut through containment B -> restore main power -> answer the intercom ->
reach the loading yard.

Set pieces: a gas-flooded corridor where the muzzle flash is the problem; a
blackout in containment B where the only light is the tank glow; a floor that
goes; Haldane's voice arriving on the intercom, calm, mid-experiment.

**Boss:** SUBJECT V-09 "EL JARDINERO" — the specimen handler, third-generation
growth, in the containment atrium.

**Story turn:** in the Act 1 safe room, Callen finds the incision at the base of
Elena's neck. He does not tell her what it means. Kowalski does, over the radio,
because Kowalski will not lie to a twenty-one-year-old.

### ACT II — "ASH AND RAIN"
**Sections:** `motor_pool`, `chapel_road`, `chapel`, `causeway`,
`drowned_village`, `cistern`, `cannery`
**Objective chain:** cross the motor pool -> raise the mast for comms ->
take the causeway at low tide -> recover the sluice keys -> drain the cistern ->
cut through the cannery -> settle with Merse.

Weather becomes an enemy. Standing rain, 20 m visibility, lightning that
whites out the aim. Open arenas (motor pool, village square) alternate with
drowning corridors (cistern, cannery cold rooms).

**Bosses:** LA MADRE (brood mass in the flooded cistern) and COL. VIKTOR MERSE
(cannery kill-floor, armour, three squads, a very bad attitude about money).

**Story turn:** Elena reads the tide table on the causeway wall and tells Callen
he has eleven minutes, not forty. She is right. It is the first time he does
something because she said so.

### ACT III — "THE PERIMETER"
**Sections:** `sea_cliffs`, `catacombs`, `ossuary`, `trestle_bridge`, `docks`,
`helipad`
**Objective chain:** climb the cliffs -> cross the catacombs -> end Serrano ->
burn the archive (optional) -> cross the trestle before it goes -> signal from
the dock lamp -> hold the helipad -> board.

**Extraction clock starts** the moment they clear the cliff line: CARRION-6
launches into a storm with a hard fuel deck. Everything after this is timed and
the HUD says so.

**Bosses:** FATHER AMADOR SERRANO (ossuary), then THE HARVEST — Serrano's
terminal expression — on the helipad in the rotor wash.

**Story turn:** in the catacombs, Callen uses her name.

## 6. OPTIONAL CONTENT (drives endings)

**Survivors (3):**
- **JUNE OKONKWO**, ARGOS comms tech — welded into a locker in the power
  station. Act 1. Talks too much because she has not spoken in four days.
- **CPL. IVO STANEK**, Merse's man, deserter — cannery cold room, femur broken,
  three rounds left, aiming them at himself when found. Act 2.
- **TOMAS ARELLANO**, 11 — village bell tower. Does not speak. His drawing is
  a findable document, tagged as evidence by someone who did not look at it.

**Research data:** the archive core in the ossuary annex. Burning it removes
Verdigris from the world's reach. Not burning it means ARGOS has a copy on the
mainland by Thursday.

**Haldane's fate:** three resolutions, all available.
- **SPARED, EXTRACTED** — she walks onto the helicopter and testifies. The
  inhibitor works. The paper trail becomes a hearing.
- **SPARED, LEFT** — she stays with the archive to guarantee the burn. Dies
  clean. Gives Callen both doses.
- **KILLED** — she deserved it, and the second dose burns with her.

**Inhibitor doses:** one for Elena is mandatory to avoid the worst ending. The
second dose is the difference between an A and an S.

## 10. RADIO DESIGN

Forty-eight entries. Three registers:

- **Procedural** (ANVIL): timings, grids, thresholds. "WARDEN, ANVIL. Mark your
  last known. I have you drifting east of the plot."
- **Human** (ANVIL, quiet stretches): "You have not asked me about the abort
  criteria. I am aware you read them."
- **Cockpit** (CARRION-6): weather, fuel, altitude, and one running joke about
  a thermos, which pays off in the final transmission.

CARRION-6 goes silent for a stretch in Act 2 (mast down). Getting him back is an
objective. His return line is deliberately the warmest in the script.

---

## 11. ENEMY VOCAL DESIGN

Cult hosts keep language. That is the horror: they are *arguing about you*.
Lines are short, shouted, transliterated Spanish, ASCII-safe. No subtitles —
they are texture. Key repeated phrases the player will learn to fear:

- `"Semilla! La semilla camina!"` — *The seed walks.* Spoken when Elena is seen.
  Player learns: this means they are coming for **her**, not him.
- `"Detras! Detras de ti!"` — flank call. Always true.
- `"Por el Padre."` — spoken before a suicide charge.
- `"No la toques. Es del Padre."` — brutes will body-block rather than hit her.

Brutes: guttural, two syllables max. Shielders: territorial, static. Spitters:
wet, sing-song, childlike. Crawlers: no language left — the loss is the point.

---

## 12. QUARTERMASTER ("PEDDLER")

RE4's merchant, reworked as a pirate radio frequency. **PEDDLER** is a smuggler
who has been running supply caches on Isla Verdadera for nine years and is
currently trapped on it. He sells from cached drums, takes payment in ARGOS
scrip and ammunition, and never once asks what is happening.

Voice: amused, transactional, unbothered. Never scared. His last line of the
game, if the player has bought enough, is the only unambiguous kindness a
stranger shows Callen.

---

## 13. THEMATIC CONTRACT

- **Isolation:** the radio is the only proof of an outside world, and it is
  degraded, intermittent, and eventually lost.
- **The weight of one life:** the game never gives Callen a squad. Every system —
  the command wheel, the shared inventory, her grabbed-scream audio cue — exists
  to make one person feel like a load-bearing responsibility rather than a
  follower AI.
- **The cost of bio-weapons:** nobody in this story is a monster on purpose.
  Haldane has a waiver. Merse has an invoice. ARGOS has a contract. The paperwork
  is the villain and it is all findable.
- **Human resilience:** Elena's ladder is the whole thesis. She does not become
  a soldier. She becomes *someone who acts*, which is different and harder, and
  the last thing she does in the game is a choice nobody asked her to make.

## 14. FINAL IMAGE

Rotor wash. Rain going sideways. The island going dark behind them one string of
lights at a time. Elena, wrapped in a survival blanket, still holding the empty
magazine she took off him forty minutes ago because he told her to hold it and
nobody has told her to let go.

Callen: "You can put it down."

Elena: "In a minute."

Cut.
