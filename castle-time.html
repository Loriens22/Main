CASTLE TIME

It is 2026. The lattice found something: a scroll of impossible mathematics, catalogued once and never again, burned out of history on the night of 17 October 1283. You have a Motorola Timeport, a laser blaster, and roughly nine hours before the fire.

You do not have time to change out of your clothes. That will turn out to matter.

Field Manual

Operating the traveller

Move

WASD walk · Shift sprint · Space jump
Touch: left stick, RUN, JUMP

Look & Fire

Mouse to aim, click to fire the blaster. R vents heat early.
Touch: drag the right half of the screen, FIRE button

Interact

E on anything that glows — levers, chests, corpses, oddities.
Touch: USE button (it pulses when something is in reach)

Weapon modes

1 PULSE — fast, accurate.
2 LANCE — slow, punches through crowds and doors.
Q cycles. Both share one heat sink.

Heat

The blaster was never meant for sustained fire. Overheat it and it locks until it cools. Burst, don't hold.
